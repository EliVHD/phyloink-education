Setup and assumptions

AB/ab × ab/ab; shared recombination fraction r = 10% (whole-percentage steps). Each slash separates the parent's two homologous chromosomes; each chromosome lists locus A then locus B. Reproducible sample: 100 offspring, seed 1.

Synthetic diploid autosomal two-locus model, two alleles per locus, equal segregation, symmetric gamete probabilities, independent parental gamete draws, random fertilization and complete dominance at each locus. One shared recombination fraction applies to both parents; it is not a crossover count, physical distance or map-distance estimate. No sex-specific recombination, mutation, selection, segregation distortion, epistasis, penetrance variation, environment or multigeneration breeding. A_ means AA or Aa; B_ means BB or Bb. Dominance does not increase transmission. No organisms or genotypes are inferred from a tree.
