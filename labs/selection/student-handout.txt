Changing heterozygote fitness

Predict both pre-sampling frequencies before running. Compare explicit genotype fitnesses, no-drift references and finite allele counts. This synthetic comparison is a reproducible demonstration, not a forecast for real populations.

Both conditions: constant N = 100; 100 A and 100 a copies initially; 40 generations; 4 trials per condition (8 total), master seed 1. Relative AA/Aa/aa fitness: S1 = 1.2/1.1/1; S2 = 1.2/1.4/1. All six weights are explicit and fixed through time.

Synthetic diploid autosomal A/a locus; closed constant-size population, random mating and non-overlapping generations. Expected random-mating genotype proportions are weighted by each condition’s fixed positive relative genotype fitnesses before 2N binomial gene-copy sampling. Fitness weights are relative contributions, not survival percentages or phenotype-dominance assignments. No individual genotypes, survival events or pedigrees are tracked. Conditions share N and initial A copies, but use separate seeded streams; matching trial numbers are not paired individuals or guaranteed counterfactuals. No mutation, migration, linked loci, changing environment, size changes, sex structure, zero-fitness extinction or empirical inference. Missing alleles stay absent; locus loss/fixation is not species extinction. Model N is an input, not an estimate of real populations. No traits or fitnesses are inferred from a tree.
